Welcome to Pilidium's Local Minecraft Server

A casual, modded Fabric 1.21.11 server built around exploration, quality-of-life, and having a good time. The world is shaped by Tectonic terrain generation with hundreds of custom structures scattered across the Overworld, a fully overhauled Nether via Incendium, and new End content. Pick an Origin with unique abilities, fast-travel with Waystones, brew drinks at the Brewery, and enjoy conveniences like tree-felling, crop right-click harvesting, per-player loot, and no anvil cost cap.

Non-consensual griefing/attacking is highly discouraged. We are a relatively peaceful bunch of people trying to enjoy the game in our own ways.

This dashboard lists every client and server mod with descriptions, all config files, server properties, active gamerules, player statistics, and a beginner's guide to get you started.

Beginner's Guide — How This Server Differs from Vanilla

This server runs a curated set of mods on top of Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric). Most changes are transparent — the world just feels richer. Below is everything you need to know, in the order you will encounter it.

When You First Join

EasyAuth — Server Login
The server uses its own authentication. The first time you join you will be asked to /register a password. On future logins you will need to /login before you can move.
Example: /register mypassword mypassword  →  then on reconnect: /login mypassword
Origins: Legacy — Choose Your Origin
Right after spawning you will see a selection screen. Pick an Origin — each one gives you unique abilities and trade-offs. For example, the Avian can slow-fall but is scared of the dark, while the Merling breathes underwater but cannot survive on land for long. You can change your origin later with the /origin set command if the server allows it.

Gameplay Changes

FallingTree — Instant Tree Felling
Break one log of a tree and the entire tree comes down at once, dropping all its logs. No more pillaring up 30 blocks to get the top of a jungle tree.
Just break the bottom log with an axe — the whole tree falls.
Carry On — Pick Up & Move Blocks
Shift + right-click a chest, spawner, or even a mob to pick it up and carry it. Right-click again to place it back down. Great for reorganising bases or relocating villagers.
Sneak + right-click a chest → it goes on your back → place it somewhere else.
Harvest With Ease — Right-Click Harvesting
Right-click a fully grown crop to harvest and auto-replant it in one action. No need to break and re-place seeds.
Walk through your wheat farm holding right-click — everything gets harvested and replanted.
No Expensive — No Anvil Cap
In vanilla, the anvil refuses repairs/renames after the cost exceeds 39 levels (“Too Expensive!”). This mod removes that cap, so you can keep repairing your favourite tools.
Lootr — Per-Player Loot
Loot chests in dungeons, temples, and shipwrecks are unique per player. Everyone gets their own loot — no more racing to be first. Lootr chests have a sparkle effect so you can tell them apart.
Two players open the same dungeon chest → each sees different, untouched loot.
Chunk Loaders — Keep Areas Active
Craft a Chunk Loader block to keep nearby chunks loaded even when no players are around. Useful for farms, redstone machines, and mob grinders that need to run 24/7.
Beacon Range Extender
Beacons have a larger effective range than vanilla, so you can cover your entire base with a single beacon.
Brewery — Craft Drinks
Adds a full brewing system using cauldrons, brewing stands, and barrels. Cook ingredients in a cauldron over fire, distil in a brewing stand, then age in wooden barrels to produce drinks with various effects.
Place a cauldron over fire → add wheat → wait 8 min → bottle it → distil → age in a barrel → wheat beer.

Travel & Navigation

Waystones — Fast Travel
You will find Waystones scattered around the world (often in villages). Right-click one to activate it, then right-click any other Waystone to teleport there. You can also craft your own.
Find a Waystone in a village → activate it → teleport back to it from any other Waystone.
Xaero's Minimap & World Map
A minimap shows in the corner of your screen with nearby terrain, mobs, and players. Press M to open a full-screen world map of everywhere you have explored. You can set waypoints by pressing B.
Press B to create a waypoint at your current location. Press M to see the full map.
Bobby — Extended Render Distance
Bobby caches chunks on your computer so you can see far beyond the server's view distance. The server might send 10 chunks, but Bobby remembers and renders chunks you have already visited.

The World Looks Different

The terrain, biomes, and structures are heavily enhanced. The world still feels like Minecraft, but exploration is far more rewarding.

Tectonic — Terrain Overhaul
Terrain generation is dramatically different: towering mountains, deep valleys, winding rivers, and vast cave systems. The landscape feels much more epic and varied than vanilla.
Promenade — New Biomes & Mobs
Adds new biomes like cherry oak forests, dark amaranth forests (Nether), and glacarian taiga. Some come with unique mobs and building blocks.
Incendium — Nether Overhaul
The Nether is completely reimagined with new biomes, structures, mobs, and loot. Expect huge fortresses, volcanic caves, and quartz palaces.
Structures Everywhere
A large collection of structure mods populates the world with things to find: Structory (ruins, towers), Explorify (small vanilla-style builds), Dungeons & Taverns (underground dungeons and roadside taverns), ChoiceTheorem's Overhauled Village (completely redesigned villages for every biome), Towns & Towers (pillager outpost variants), and the Moogs series (End, Nether, sky, voyager, temple, and village structures). Exploration is the main loop — there is always something new over the next hill.

UI & Information

JEI — Just Enough Items
Press E to open your inventory and you will see a searchable item list on the right. Click any item to see its crafting recipe. Press U on an item to see what it is used in. Essential for looking up recipes from modded content.
Hover over an item → press R to see how to craft it, U to see what uses it.
WTHIT — “What The Hell Is That”
A small tooltip at the top of the screen tells you what block or mob you are looking at, including its mod source and, for some blocks, extra info like crop growth stage or chest contents.
Enchantment Descriptions
Hover over an enchanted item and the tooltip now shows a plain-English description of what each enchantment does. No more guessing what “Impaling V” means.
BetterF3
The F3 debug screen is redesigned to be colour-coded and easier to read, with information grouped into clear sections.
Chat Heads
Chat messages show the sender's player head icon next to their name, making it easier to tell who said what.
Zoomify — Zoom Key
Press C to zoom in like a spyglass, without needing one. Works just like the old OptiFine zoom.
Villager Names
Villagers have random human names displayed above their heads, making it easier to keep track of your villager trading hall.
Iris Shaders + Sodium
Sodium replaces the rendering engine for much better FPS, and Iris lets you load shader packs (like Complementary or BSL) for beautiful lighting, water, and shadows. Press K to toggle shaders on/off.

Vanilla Tweaks & Datapacks

Several datapacks add quality-of-life features and extra recipes without any client mods needed. These are all server-side — they just work.

Graves
When you die, your items are stored in a grave block at the death location instead of scattering on the ground. Walk up to it and right-click (or just walk over it) to reclaim everything. No more losing items in lava!
/back
Type /trigger back in chat to teleport to your last death location. Handy for getting back to your grave quickly.
/tpa (Teleport Request)
Request a teleport to another player: /trigger tpa then follow the prompts. The other player must accept before you are teleported.
AFK Display
If you stand still for a while your name turns grey in the tab list so others know you are AFK.
Villager Workstation Highlights
When you look at a villager, their linked workstation block gets a glowing outline, making it easy to figure out which workstation belongs to which villager.
XP Bottling
Stand on an enchanting table and throw a glass bottle to convert your XP into a Bottle o' Enchanting. Great for storing experience safely.
Rotation Wrenches
Craft a Redstone Rotation Wrench or Terracotta Rotation Wrench to rotate redstone components and glazed terracotta blocks in-place without breaking them.
Orb of Origin Recipe
Normally you can only pick your Origin once. A custom recipe lets you craft an Orb of Origin (expensive — requires a Nether Star, Netherite Ingot, Mace, Eye of Ender, Heart of the Sea, Shulker Box, and Echo Shard) to re-choose your Origin.

Extra Crafting Recipes (Vanilla Tweaks bundle)

Craftable Blackstone — Smelt basalt to get blackstone.
Copper Powered Rails — Craft powered rails with copper ingots instead of gold.
Rotten Flesh to Leather — Smelt rotten flesh into leather.
Powder to Glass — Smelt concrete powder directly into stained glass.
Sandstone Dyeing — Dye sandstone variants between regular and red.
Universal Dyeing — Re-dye already-dyed blocks (wool, concrete, beds, etc.).
Wool to String — Place wool in a crafting grid to get string.
Uncraft Ice — Craft packed/blue ice back into regular ice.
Uncraft Nether Wart Block — Craft nether wart blocks back into nether wart.
More Stairs — Stair recipes for additional block types.
Slabs & Stairs to Blocks — Combine slabs or stairs back into full blocks.

Behind the Scenes (Server Performance)

You do not need to think about these, but they are why the server runs smoothly:

Lithium — Optimises game logic, mob AI, and world ticking.
C2ME — Multi-threaded chunk loading for faster terrain generation.
FerriteCore — Reduces the server's memory usage.
Krypton — Optimises the networking stack for lower latency.
Alternate Current — Faster redstone calculations.
AntiXray — Hides ores from X-ray cheats.
PacketFixer — Prevents disconnects from oversized packets.
Sparse Structures — Spreads structures out so they don't cluster unnaturally.
